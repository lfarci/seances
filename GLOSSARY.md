# Séances

Terms for finding cinema screenings by time and location.

## Language

**Cinema**:
A named physical venue containing one or more rooms.
_Avoid_: Room, screen when referring to the venue

**Room**:
An auditorium within a cinema in which a screening takes place.
_Avoid_: Cinema when referring to an auditorium

**Film**:
The movie being shown, independent of any particular screening.
_Avoid_: Screening when referring to the movie itself

**Original title**:
The title under which a film was originally released, in its original language. It may differ from a localized title used by a cinema.
_Avoid_: English title when the film's original title is not English

**Screening**:
One scheduled showing of a film at a particular cinema and start time, with its own language and format details. Its room may be unknown.
_Avoid_: Film when referring to a scheduled showing

**Availability window**:
The period the user has available at the cinema, from earliest acceptable screening start to latest acceptable finish. Travel time is excluded.
_Avoid_: Start-time range

**Runtime**:
The duration of the film itself, excluding trailers and other pre-show material.
_Avoid_: Total screening duration

**Estimated finish**:
The predicted time a screening ends, accounting for film runtime and an allowance for initial trailers and advertising. It may differ from the actual finish.
_Avoid_: Confirmed finish when the time is derived

**Source-reported finish**:
The finish time displayed by UGC for a particular screening. It is distinct from a locally calculated estimate and does not guarantee the actual time a visitor leaves.
_Avoid_: Actual finish, guaranteed finish

**Pre-show allowance**:
The time allowed for trailers and advertising between the advertised screening start and the film itself.
_Avoid_: Runtime when referring to this allowance

**Assumed runtime**:
A provisional three-hour film duration used when the actual runtime cannot be found. It is neither a maximum film duration nor evidence that a screening fits an availability window.
_Avoid_: Known runtime, maximum runtime
